KITCONTENT — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 20 August 2026

1. Introduction

KitContent ("KitContent", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights regarding your information when you use KitContent.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, KitContent may process:

Account Information

This may include information required to identify or authenticate your account.

Content

We may process content you create or submit through KitContent, including:

  • text;
  • captions;
  • hashtags;
  • generated content;
  • images;
  • content metadata; and
  • generated media files.

TikTok Information

If you connect a TikTok account, KitContent may receive information made available through TikTok's authorization and APIs.

This may include:

  • TikTok account identifiers;
  • authorization tokens;
  • permitted account information;
  • publishing permissions; and
  • information necessary to publish content through the TikTok Content Posting API.

KitContent only requests permissions necessary for the features it provides.

Technical Information

We may automatically receive limited technical information such as:

  • IP address;
  • browser type;
  • device information;
  • operating system;
  • timestamps;
  • error logs; and
  • basic usage information.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • operate KitContent;
  • generate content requested by users;
  • generate and process images;
  • store generated content;
  • provide content management features;
  • connect third-party services;
  • publish content to connected social media accounts;
  • maintain security;
  • diagnose technical problems;
  • prevent abuse; and
  • improve the service.

We do not use information for purposes unrelated to providing and improving KitContent unless permitted or required by law.

4. Artificial Intelligence Services

KitContent may send relevant user-provided content to third-party AI or image-generation providers in order to provide requested features.

These providers may process information according to their respective terms and privacy policies.

KitContent does not intentionally send TikTok account passwords to AI providers.

5. TikTok Integration

If you connect TikTok to KitContent, authorization information may be processed to enable publishing functionality.

KitContent uses TikTok's official developer APIs.

KitContent does not request your TikTok password.

You can disconnect the TikTok integration where the feature is available.

Disconnecting an account may prevent KitContent from performing future actions on that account.

6. Access Tokens

When you authorize a third-party integration, KitContent may securely store authentication tokens required to maintain that connection.

These tokens are used only for authorized functionality.

Access tokens are not intentionally displayed publicly or exposed through the KitContent frontend.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide the service;
  • maintain user-created content;
  • maintain security;
  • comply with legal obligations; or
  • resolve disputes.

Users may request deletion of applicable personal information, subject to legal and operational requirements.
